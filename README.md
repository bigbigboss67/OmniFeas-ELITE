# OmniFeas ELITE — $1M UNIDO & Bank-Ready Feasibility Engine with Live AI Avatar

![OmniFeas Elite](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1573496359142-b8d87734a5a2?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=85)

OmniFeas Elite is a next-generation autonomous investment appraisal and credit underwriting engine designed to meet the rigorous feasibility standards of the **United Nations Industrial Development Organization (UNIDO Volumes I–III)**, commercial bank credit committees, and government industrial funds (e.g. SIDF, Kafalah).

---

## 🌟 Key Architecture & Capabilities

### 1. Step 1: Generative Feasibility Prompt Console
- **Natural Language Input with Voice**: Enter custom project parameters via typing or speech recognition (`🎙️`).
- **16 Pre-Calibrated Universal Sectors**:
  - Solar Cleantech EPC, Specialty Coffee Roastery, Daycare Surgery Clinic, Enterprise B2B SaaS, Cold-Chain Logistics, Hydroponic AgriTech, Dark Kitchens, Plastic Recycling, Boutique Eco-Resorts, EV Ultra-Fast Charging, Precast Concrete Fabrication, Automated Fulfillment, Private STEM K-12 Academy, Generic Pharma Formulation, FinTech Payment Gateways, and AI Tier-IV Data Centers.
- **Deterministic Math Engine**: Sub-300ms calculation of 10 UNIDO modules, 5-year financial statements, debt amortizations, and covenant metrics.

### 2. Step 2: Multi-Underwriter Live Video Committee
- **60 FPS HTML5 Canvas Viseme Lip-Sync**: Real-time audio FFT frequency spectrum analysis driving viseme mouth shapes (A, E, I, O, U, consonants, jaw drops).
- **Procedural Life Simulation**: Realistic periodic eye blinking and breathing rise-and-fall physics.
- **3-Underwriter Committee Switcher**:
  - 👩‍💼 **Dr. Sarah Sterling**: Senior UNIDO Feasibility Economist (Financial modeling, Min DSCR $\ge 1.35\text{x}$, debt amortizations).
  - 👨‍💼 **Eng. Tariq Al-Mansoor**: Chief Technical & CAPEX Director (Plant sizing, machinery procurement, civil works).
  - 👩‍🔬 **Dr. Elena Rostova**: Senior ESG & Risk Matrix Specialist (Environmental clearance, carbon exposure, default switching).
- **Two-Way Covenant Binding**: The avatar actively interrupts if the **Revenue Shock Slider** breaches the bank covenant floor ($< 1.35\text{x}$).

### 3. Step 3: Embeddable Portal Widget Architecture
- **Web Component**: `<omnifeas-portal>` drop-in script for host banking portals, venture accelerators, and incubators.
- **React / Next.js SDK**: Full component and hook support.
- **Headless JSON REST API**: Standardized underwriting request/response schemas.
- **Live Host Bank Simulator**: Demo interface simulating a real commercial loan origination system.

### 4. Step 4: Multi-Format Financial Model & Report Exporter
- **Client-Side CSV Model**: Formatted 5-year financial statement download with live EBITDA, Debt Service, and Cash Flow calculations.
- **Print-Ready UNIDO Memorandum**: Black-and-white optimized PDF appraisal dossier.
